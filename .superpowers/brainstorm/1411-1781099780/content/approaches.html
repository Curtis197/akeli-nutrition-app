Exclude member — 3 approaches

How should the admin trigger the exclusion from the members list?

A

Trailing "more" icon on each row Recommended

A ⋮ (kebab) icon appears on the right of each member row when the viewer is admin. Tapping it shows a small menu: "Exclure du groupe". Clean, discoverable, matches most group-management UIs (WhatsApp, Discord).

[ 👤 Fatou Koné  Admin        ✉   ⋮ ]
B

Long-press to reveal actions

Long-pressing a member row opens a bottom sheet with actions: "Voir le profil", "Message privé", "Exclure du groupe". Only shows exclusion when viewer is admin. Less discoverable — users may not know to long-press.

[ 👤 Mamadou Diallo              ✉ ]
   ↳ long-press → bottom sheet
C

Swipe-to-reveal (admin only)

Swiping left on a member row reveals a red "Exclure" button, visible only to admins. Familiar iOS-style pattern but requires a swipeable list widget and can conflict with scroll gestures.
